Martian Map & Explorer Survival Guide

BioRadius answers one question live: “Am I still inside the safe return radius — and what changed?”

SAFE T ≤ 0.75

≥ 25% reserve margin remains. Keep exploring.

CAUTION 0.75 < T ≤ 1.0

Approaching the turnaround limit. Start heading home.

EXCEEDED T > 1.0

Turn back now and follow the blue return path.

1 · Safe-Return Status

  • SAFE — round-trip cost leaves ≥ 25% margin across O₂, battery, calories, and radiation. The status readout shows +% MARGIN and km to turnaround.
  • CAUTION — nearing the reserve limit (default startup: 350 kcal, ~16% margin). Begin the return traverse before hitting 100%.
  • EXCEEDED — turn back immediately. The status readout prints exact -kcal / -% O₂ deficits; jettison samples or abort.

2 · Reading the 3D Map

  • Green fill + amber ring — safe interior (T < 0.75) easing into the caution buffer.
  • Thick red bands — impassable > 33° cliffs; the solver routes around them when HiRISE Slope is on.
  • Red perimeter (T = 1.0) — minimum return boundary; turns red in the No-Return zone.
  • Blue path (#0A84FF) — lowest-energy route to Base (75, 75). Drag the pin or tap a waypoint beacon to replan.

3 · Five NASA Layers

Each layer button along the bottom of the screen recolors the terrain, toggles that hazard in routing, and shows live safe-area impact:

  • Steep Cliffs · HiRISE — blocks > 33° scarps; models uphill/downhill work at 3.71 m/s².
  • Sand & Cold · THEMIS — drift-sand slip (TIU < 100 ⇒ 1.85×) and shadowed heating (165 K).
  • Sample Load · CRISM — carried rock mass (82 + 50 + sample kg); uncheck to simulate jettison.
  • Wind Drag · MEDA — katabatic headwind/tailwind drag (8.5 m/s).
  • Radiation · RAD — 36-azimuth canyon shielding plus live DONKI SEP storm alerts.

4 · Watch, Sliders & Vitals

  • Wayfinder dial — bezel shows mission timer and Home bearing; rings track O₂ and Battery/Calories. Sliders set Heart Rate (60–190), Suit + Sample (50–120 kg), Calories (300–2000); Collect +15 kg adds a core sample; Quick Waypoint Jump moves the pin.
  • 1 Hz Eco Mode — pauses the 3D GPU (0 mW) and updates the watch once per second, stretching battery from 2.8 → 15.6 hrs.
  • Crew Health panel — live Heart Strain (HRR > 75%), SpO₂, core temp (> 37.5°C), suit CO₂ (> 2.5 mmHg), and the HR vs. safe-distance trend.

5 · Zero-GPS Horizon & Audits

  • Zero-GPS lock — Mars has no GPS or magnetic compass, so BioRadius matches the visor’s 360° skyline to orbital DEMs to fix (GX, GY) and true-North heading (and feeds canyon radiation shielding). Click ZERO-GPS on the 3D view to inspect.
  • Data & System Check — the two buttons at the top of the screen show live NASA data provenance and verify all physics/math checks.

  • Hotkeys — 1–4 replay the 4-Act story, ? toggles this guide, Esc closes panels/dialogs.
