### Partner data sources

The 5 NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) Instrument Archives

Layer 1 — MRO HiRISE Digital Terrain Models (DTEEC) and MGS MOLA Global Elevation (MEGDR)  
HiRISE PDS Archive (USGS / Univ. of Arizona): https://www.uahirise.org/dtm/  
PDS Geosciences Node (MRO HiRISE and MOLA): https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mro/hirise.htm  
MGS MOLA MEGDR Archive: https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mgs/megdr.html  

Layer 2 — 2001 Mars Odyssey THEMIS Thermal Inertia (TIU)  
PDS Geosciences / Mars Odyssey THEMIS Archive: https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/odyssey/themis.html  
ASU Mars Space Flight Facility THEMIS Data Portal: https://themis.asu.edu/  

Layer 3 — MRO CRISM Spectral Summary Products (Hydrated Sulfates and Phyllosilicates)  
PDS Geosciences Node — CRISM Archive: https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mro/crism.htm  

Layer 4 — Curiosity REMS and Perseverance MEDA Surface Wind  
PDS Atmospheres Node — MSL REMS Archive: https://atmos.nmsu.edu/data_and_services/atmospheres_data/MARS/curiosity/rems.html  
PDS Atmospheres Node — Mars 2020 MEDA Archive: https://atmos.nmsu.edu/PDS/data/PDS4/Mars2020/mars2020_meda/  

Layer 5 — Curiosity RAD (Radiation Assessment Detector) Surface Dose Archive  
PDS Planetary Plasma Interactions (PPI) Node — MSL RAD: https://pds-ppi.igpp.ucla.edu/search/?sc=Mars_Science_Laboratory&i=RAD
